About this Match

A 20 over match between two sides at Marchant Park, Chermside. This match includes two club umpires and a proper scorer. This is a amatuer level game, however it will be a friendly vibe around; everyone gets a bat or a bowl.

Whites are preferred but not enforced. Bring your own equipment if you have them, there will be limited spare kits and helmets. Toss is at 8:30am sharp.

Additional Information before you show up

Whites - if you have them, spikes or normal runners, a hat and sunscreen, and lots and lots of water. Stumps, balls, helmets and two spare club kits are provided.

Wet Weather calls are made by 7am on the morning of the match once the curator has had a look at the pitch. If the match is cancelled, the host will update the status and players and spectators will be notified via their preferred contact method.

Free parking provided at the field, and street parking available as well. There is also a bus stop 5 mins away or at Westfield Chermside.
