A Acknowledgement of country is a formal way of paying respects to the indigenous people of Australia. It is different from a welcome to country where the Traditional Owners deliver a welcome to the land. Acknowledgement of Country can be offered by anyone to show their respect.

There are a vast number of Aboriginal groups across Australia and it can be confusing to know what ground you stand on. This page is aimed at helping you learn or create your own Acknowledgement of Country. Some examples of groups include:

  • Brisbane City: Yuggera
  • Sydney: Eora
  • Canberra: Ngunawal
  • Melbourne: Woiworung and Boonwurrung
  • Hobart: Nuenonne
  • Adelaide: Kaurna
  • Perth: Wajuk
  • Darwin: Larrakia

    • While no offical format exists, a traditional message to custodians is typically formatted as:

    I would like to begin by acknowledging the Traditional Owners of the land on which we meet today, the (people) of the (nation) and pay my respects to Elders past and present.
